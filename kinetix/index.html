KINETIX ORGANIZATION

ABOUT US

Made to bring people together

Kinetix is a student-led event management organization made up of fourth-year Bachelor of Physical Education students at City College of Angeles. Through our Event Management subject, we put our learning into practice by planning meaningful activities that encourage movement, teamwork, leadership, and active living.

Our mission

We turn ideas into impactful, well-coordinated events through creativity, collaboration, and purposeful planning.

Our vision

To be a trusted event organization creating accessible experiences that inspire action and strengthen communities.

Privacy Policy

KINETIX Organization respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when you visit our website, communicate with our organization, or participate in our events and activities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website and organization, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and other communication details
  • Event registration or participation information
  • Organization, school, or affiliation, when relevant to an event
  • Information voluntarily provided through contact forms, registration forms, surveys, or inquiries
  • Technical information necessary for the operation and security of the website

We only request information that is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided to KINETIX may be used to:

  • Process event registrations and participation
  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Communicate important event announcements and updates
  • Coordinate programs, workshops, seminars, sports activities, and other events
  • Improve our services, programs, and website
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of our website
  • Comply with applicable school policies and legal requirements

We will not use your personal information for unrelated purposes without appropriate notice or consent.

3. Sharing of Information

KINETIX does not sell or rent personal information. Information may only be shared when reasonably necessary to conduct an event or activity, fulfill a requested service, comply with applicable requirements, or when you have provided appropriate permission. When an activity involves third-party partners, sponsors, venues, or service providers, information will only be shared when necessary for the relevant activity and subject to applicable privacy requirements.

4. Photographs and Event Documentation

KINETIX may document its events through photographs, videos, or other media for legitimate organizational purposes, such as event documentation, reports, and promotional materials. Where required, appropriate consent or permission will be obtained before using identifiable photographs or recordings, particularly for promotional purposes. If you have concerns regarding the use of your image or personal information, you may contact KINETIX through the contact details provided on our website.

5. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive or confidential information through public or unsecured channels.

6. Data Retention

Personal information will only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, comply with applicable requirements, or support legitimate organizational activities. When information is no longer necessary, appropriate measures may be taken to securely delete, destroy, or anonymize it.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, regulations, and school policies, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Know what personal information is being collected and how it is used
  • Request access to information concerning you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Raise concerns regarding the handling of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

For privacy-related concerns, please contact KINETIX through the contact information provided on this website.

KINETIX may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in our activities, website, policies, or applicable requirements. Any significant changes will be communicated through the website or other appropriate channels.
